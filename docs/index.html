Our story

Same family recipes. New Florida home.

Pieconic started as a family bakery in Chatham, New York, in the heart of the Hudson Valley, crimping every crust by hand with local fruit and no shortcuts.

Now we're bringing those recipes south to America's oldest city, adding the Cuban and Puerto Rican-style empanadas we grew up on, and building a shop where families come back again and again.
